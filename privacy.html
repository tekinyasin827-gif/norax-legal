Privacy Policy (English)

Summary: Blastrix is a fully offline puzzle game. It never connects to a server and does not collect, store, process or share any personal data.

  • Data collected: None. No account, name, email, location, device identifier, advertising identifier (IDFA) or IP address.
  • Network: The app uses no internet connection; no data ever leaves your device.
  • On-device storage: Game progress, stars, in-game gold, settings and statistics are stored only locally on your device. Nobody, including the developer, can access them; they are removed when you delete the app.
  • Ads, analytics, tracking: None. No third-party SDKs.
  • In-app purchases: None. In-game gold has no real-world value.
  • Children: The app collects no personal data from any user, including children (COPPA / GDPR-K).
  • GDPR / KVKK: We process no personal data about you. If you email us for support, your email is used only to reply and is deleted on request.
